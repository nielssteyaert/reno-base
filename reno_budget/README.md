# Renovation Budget

![Supports aarch64][aarch64-shield] ![Supports amd64][amd64-shield]

Keep your home renovation on budget, right inside Home Assistant.

- Expenses from estimate → quote → ordered → invoiced → paid, with installment payments
- Categories with subcategories, plus rooms, each with an optional budget
- VAT per expense (amounts entered with or without VAT)
- Dashboard with budget consumption, contingency, payments due and milestones
- Reports: budget vs actual, spending over time, forecast, supplier breakdown
- Timeline of planned vs actual milestones
- Attach quotes, invoices and photos
- Import from Excel/CSV, export to Excel
- Backups with one-click restore, and a full change history
